# German Words in Connections

A short glossary of German demonyms and loanwords with hidden English endings, using the November 20, 2026 New York Times Connections puzzle as a worked example. For the German meaning, pronunciation, and grammar of *Stuttgarter*, see [Stuttgarter Meaning](https://stuttgartermeaning.com/).

## How the wordplay works

In puzzle #1258, `STUTTGARTER` belongs in a group whose words end in names for types of lingerie. A *Stuttgarter* is someone from Stuttgart, and its final six letters spell `GARTER`, the English word for a band that holds up a stocking. The German word itself is formed by adding `-er` to *Stuttgart*.

To test this pattern in a puzzle, read the final letters of several tiles as standalone words. Check whether those shorter words share a category. A single recognisable ending is easy to find by chance.
